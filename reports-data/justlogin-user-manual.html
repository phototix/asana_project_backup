JustLogin User Manual

View-only operational guide for the KMSPKS production tenant. Covers login, dashboard, attendance, people, settings, and user administration.

Download the manual

The PDF is a 53-page A4 guide with numbered steps, screenshots, safety callouts, and a page index. Personal and employee data has been masked in the screenshots.

Open PDF manual Download PDF Download editable DOCX

The DOCX is the editable working copy, including an “Editing this working copy” page and revision-history table. The PDF is the published copy.

View-only: no production data was created, updated, deleted, submitted, approved, exported, or downloaded while preparing this manual.
Training: use a non-production tenant and test accounts for hands-on practice. Do not follow add, edit, or remove steps in production without approval.

What the manual covers

  • Sign in, Forgot Password, SSO, profile menu, My Settings, and logout
  • Dashboard, Action Center, Customize, and company links
  • Attendance: Dashboard, Approval Central, Roster, Reports, Administration, and Configuration
  • People: profile, Employees directory, More Actions, Approvals, OED, and OED History
  • Settings: Employee Fields, Employee ID, Company Directory, Share Company Access, Training, Access Levels, Approvals, Public Holiday, Dashboard Configuration, and Custom Notifications
  • User Administration: Add User and Employee Roles
  • Common add/edit/remove guidance, known limitations, and screenshot index

Important notes

  • The manual is a reference, not an authorisation to change production records.
  • Screenshots have names, emails, phone numbers, and other personal data masked.
  • Some pages showed empty states or known limitations during capture; these are recorded in the manual.
  • Use the Help icon in JustLogin or the organisation’s support channel for issues not covered by the manual.
