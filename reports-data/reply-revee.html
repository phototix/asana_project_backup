1. Alignment position

Target launch date

The target launch date will be determined by Marcomm following internal alignment and once the necessary requirements and readiness criteria have been addressed.

Ivan and Brandon will continue supporting technical readiness, coordinating with Codigo and tracking outstanding issues that may affect the launch.

Overall Asana ticket handling

  • Items within the agreed project scope should remain with Codigo for review and resolution.
  • Marcomm and the respective users should review and prioritise business requirements.
  • Codigo should advise on technical feasibility, estimated timelines and any potential CR implications.
  • For E2E, the focus should remain on identifying and resolving defects against the agreed requirements, rather than introducing new enhancements.

AWS WAF

Codigo should provide further clarification on the following:

  • What alternative solutions are available to Cloudflare Turnstile or Google reCAPTCHA for bot protection?
  • What is the proposed WAF rule scope and coverage?
  • Is the implementation included in the existing project scope, or would it incur additional charges?
  • What is the potential impact on OTP API latency and false-positive rates?
  • What are the proposed rollback arrangements and ongoing monitoring responsibilities?

IT will review the technical considerations once the information is available.

Data migration

We would like to arrange a discussion to review the proposed mobile number classification, going through the respective groups individually.

Based on the current migration process, records that were successfully imported are accounted for. However, the failed records still require further assessment and a proposed solution from Codigo.

In particular, we need to understand how users can retrieve or enquire about their past activity records associated with the failed migration records. Codigo should share the latest migration breakdown and proposed approach ahead of the discussion. Any final migration decisions will require alignment with the relevant business stakeholders.
