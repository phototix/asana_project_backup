Common situations and what to do / 常见情况及处理方式

The following situations are common. They usually relate to data, filters or role permissions rather than a system fault.

以下为常见情况，通常与数据、筛选或角色权限有关，而不一定是系统故障。

  • If the Dharma School page shows “No Courses Available”, the admission window may be closed or courses may not yet be published. Check with the Dharma School team before advising the public. 若佛学班页面显示“暂无课程”，招生期可能已关闭或课程尚未发布。在告知公众前，请先向佛学班团队确认。
  • If KMS Members or Student Records is empty, clear the filters and search again. If it is still empty, ask the CMS administrator to confirm your access. 若 KMS 会员或学生记录为空，请清除筛选后重新搜索。若仍为空，请 CMS 管理员确认您的访问权限。
  • If Export Mailing List or Online Payments shows “Forbidden”, your role does not include that module. Request access through your administrator or Finance. 若导出邮件列表或线上付款显示“禁止访问”，表示您的角色不包含该模块。请通过管理员或财务申请权限。
  • If you cannot find a public page in the CMS, it may be static or maintained by another team. Contact the CMS administrator instead of editing the page directly. 若在 CMS 中找不到某个公众页面，该页面可能为静态页面或由其他团队维护。请联系 CMS 管理员，不要直接修改页面。
  • To remove content, unpublish or archive it first. Delete only with written approval. 如需移除内容，请先取消发布或归档。仅在取得书面批准后删除。

Support and escalation / 支援与上报

For access, roles, OTP or content changes, contact the CMS administrator or the owning department. Include the page URL, time, what you expected and what you saw. For public-site enquiries, use the Contact Us page or the department helpdesk.

如需访问权限、角色、验证码或内容变更，请联系 CMS 管理员或负责部门。请提供页面网址、时间、预期结果及实际结果。公众网站查询请使用“联系我们”页面或部门服务台。
